---
title: From startup to academia
summary: Before joining academia, XB spent six years (2011–2017) helping build Metropia, a University of Arizona spinoff.
---

Metropia tried to reduce congestion by changing behavior instead of building roads. Metropia predicted
future traffic, offered travelers several departure times with predicted travel times, and rewarded
them for choices that relieved congestion. A field study in Los Angeles showed promising behavior
changes and travel time savings.

XB joined as a student and founding team member in 2011 and, by the time he left in 2017, had been programmer, algorithm
developer, system architect, cloud and API engineer, routing lead, project and product manager, and
finally Director of R&D and General Manager of its subsidiary in China. Along the way he managed an
FHWA-funded study on Pay-As-You-Drive-And-You-Save (PAYDAYS) insurance, which used smartphone GPS
data and traffic context to rate driving risk.

That experience still shapes the lab: a preference for problems that matter to agencies and users,
models that run in real systems, and research that is tested in the field.
