Yuxin Ding is a Ph.D. student in Transportation Engineering at Penn State. He received his B.S. from the Civil Aviation University of China and his M.S. from the Georgia Institute of Technology. His research focuses on deploying automated vehicles in adverse conditions, such as GPS-denied environments and adverse weather, across the full stack: sensing and perception, localization, planning, and control. His work includes lightweight LiDAR-based cooperative localization for leader-follower cooperative driving automation (IEEE T-ITS, 2026). He is a recipient of the FEGR Assistantship Stipend Supplement Fund.
