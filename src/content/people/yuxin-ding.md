Yuxin Ding is a Ph.D. student in Transportation Engineering at Penn State. He received his B.S. from the Civil Aviation University of China and his M.S. from the Georgia Institute of Technology. His research focuses on autonomous vehicles and dynamic system modeling, including lightweight LiDAR-based cooperative localization for leader-follower cooperative driving automation (IEEE T-ITS, 2026). He is a recipient of the FEGR Assistantship Stipend Supplement Fund.
