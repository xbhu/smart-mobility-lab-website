Dr. Tianjia Yang is a postdoctoral scholar in the Smart Mobility Lab. His research applies AI to automated vehicles, including reinforcement learning for vehicle control and lane keeping assistance. He also leads the lab's Pennsylvania DEP project on medium- and heavy-duty zero-emission vehicles. He received his Ph.D. in Infrastructure and Environmental Systems from the University of North Carolina at Charlotte (2024), his M.S. in Transportation Planning and Management from Tongji University (2012), and his B.S. in Transportation from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (2009). His interests include connected and autonomous vehicles, machine learning, intelligent transportation systems, transportation electrification, operations research, and traffic flow theory.
