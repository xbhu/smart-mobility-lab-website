Dr. Tianjia Yang is a postdoctoral scholar in the Smart Mobility Lab, where he leads the Pennsylvania DEP project on medium- and heavy-duty zero-emission vehicles. He received his Ph.D. in Infrastructure and Environmental Systems from the University of North Carolina at Charlotte (2024), his M.S. in Transportation Planning and Management from Tongji University (2012), and his B.S. in Transportation from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (2009). His interests include intelligent transportation systems, machine learning, connected and autonomous vehicles, transportation electrification, operations research, and traffic flow theory.
