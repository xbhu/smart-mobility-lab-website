“Safer3D: Multi-View 3D Object Detection for Safer AV Perception via Spatial Feature Refinement and Salient Query Propagation,” led by former postdoc Debojyoti (Bishal) Biswas, now at Youngstown State University, with Ph.D. student Yuxin Ding, was accepted at the IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision (WACV 2027). The camera-only detector is better at finding small, distant, and partly hidden objects.
